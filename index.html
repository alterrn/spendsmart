What is SpendSmart

SpendSmart is a personal expense tracker. You record what you spend, group it into categories, and see your totals.

What you will be able to do

  • Record an expense with amount, category, date, and description
  • Edit or delete an expense
  • See your spending by category and by month
  • Create, Edit or Delete saving goal

A learning project built with Flask
